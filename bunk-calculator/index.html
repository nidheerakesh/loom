Cost of a day off

What one absence on each weekday spends, checked against what you have left.

How the numbers are worked out

  • A subject’s total for the term = classes already held + every remaining slot in your timetable.
  • You must attend ceil(total × threshold) of them, so you may miss the rest — already-missed classes are deducted first.
  • The headline figure picks real days off one at a time, always taking the day that costs the least in your tightest subject, and stops before any subject would drop under the line. It is one safe plan, not the only one.
